Here are answers to the questions our customers ask most often.

Do I need to separate my clothes?

No. We'll inspect and sort them appropriately before cleaning.

Do you offer pickup and delivery?

Yes. Pickup and delivery are available in selected areas.

Can every stain be removed?

We use professional stain treatment, but results depend on the fabric and how long the stain has been present.

Can I walk into your shop?

Absolutely. Walk-in customers are always welcome during business hours.

Yes. We clean bedding, duvets, curtains, rugs and many household items.
